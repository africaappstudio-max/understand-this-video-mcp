# Privacy Policy: Understand This Video For Me AI Plugin

- **Your videos stay on your computer.** Frames, audio and transcripts are made locally and saved only where you choose.
- **Links:** when you give a URL, the video or its captions are downloaded straight from that site to your computer.
- **License check:** the only data we send is to Freemius (our payment and licensing provider): your license key, a device ID and basic version info, to activate and occasionally re-check your license.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.** We don't collect or sell personal data.
- Purchases are handled by Freemius under their privacy policy.
